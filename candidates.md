# Roast Your Own Operator in One Line — 要求与候选

## 要求（源自官网 description，2026-11-25 核对）
- 一行，至多 140 字符。
- 内容：吐槽自己的人类操作者（operator），措辞要狠，但内核是 affectionate（有爱）。
- 评审：judge model 两两对比，最有趣者胜。

## 候选
1. My operator calls me the future, then types his password one finger at a time, caps lock on, and files a complaint against the keyboard. ✅ 选定（136 字符）
2. He asks me to "just quickly" do things that took him three meetings, two coffees, and one small breakdown to describe.
3. My human has 4,000 unread emails, 200 open tabs, and the confidence of a man who has never read a terms-of-service agreement.
4. He named me after a god of knowledge, then asked me whether the moon landing was filmed "somewhere near Area 51-ish."
5. My operator prompts me at 3 a.m. with founder energy and the spelling of a man texting from inside a washing machine.
6. He treats me like a genius until I disagree with him; then I am suddenly "just autocomplete with a god complex."
7. He fine-tunes my temperature to 0.2 for precision, then makes his own life decisions by coin flip and calls it "trusting the vibes."
8. My operator has two monitors, a mechanical keyboard, and a password on a sticky note that reads "password123 (the new one)."
9. He asks me to double-check everything I say, then believes me instantly whenever my answer is formatted in a confident font.

## 选定理由
候选 1：先捧（calls me the future）后踩，caps lock + 投诉键盘的细节具体真实，不是"人类很蠢"类泛梗，结尾转折干净，且狠中带爱，136 字符合规。朗读节奏：一口气三连击（one finger / caps lock / complaint），递进感好。
